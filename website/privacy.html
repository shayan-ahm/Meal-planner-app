Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Mealset is made by Zifab Solutions Inc. (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Mealset app.

We don’t collect your personal information

Mealset has no accounts and no sign-up. We don’t ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

Your data stays on your phone

The meals you pick, your grocery list, favourites, ratings, notes and preferences are saved only on your device. We can’t see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

No ads or tracking

Mealset has no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

App updates

To receive improvements, the app checks our update service (provided by Expo) when it opens. This sends basic technical details such as the app version and device platform, but nothing about you or your meals.

Children

Mealset is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Nutrition

Nutrition values are estimates from typical ingredients and are not medical or diet advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above.

Questions? Email mealset.help@gmail.com.
