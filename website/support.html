Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Mealset works right away with no sign-up.

How do I add meals?

On My meals, tap “Add recipes” and tap + on any recipe. Or tap “Pick for me” and Mealset chooses a varied mix.

Where is my grocery list?

Open the Grocery list tab. It combines the ingredients of all your meals, grouped by aisle. Tap an item to tick it off.

Can I avoid certain foods or allergens?

Yes. Tap Preferences at the top to set your diet, allergies and foods you don’t eat.

I got a new phone. Where are my meals?

Your meals are saved only on the phone you used, so a new phone starts fresh. Syncing between devices may come in a future update.

How accurate is the nutrition?

Nutrition values are estimates from typical ingredients and are not medical or diet advice.
